# Oddium V43 — Day Carousel

- Adds two dedicated day arrows to the betting match carousel.
- Navigation order: `⏪ Jour` • `◀ Match` • `▶ Match` • `⏩ Jour`.
- Match arrows stay inside the currently selected day.
- Day arrows move to the previous/next available match day and reset to the first fixture of that day.
- Dates are grouped in Europe/Paris local time.
- The same private Discord page is edited; no extra navigation page is created.
- Simple/combo selection and the dedicated combo ticket remain unchanged.
